Questions parents ask

Which phones and tablets work?

Little Cubbie Playtime runs on compatible phones and tablets that can read NFC, the same tap technology used for contactless payments. The quick setup guide shows exactly what you need.

Do I need the Cubbie Box too?

No. A cubbie and the Little Cubbie Playtime app are all you need. The Cubbie Box is an optional speaker for completely screen-free play.

What does one cubbie unlock?

30+ stories, songs, games and activities about that character, ready the moment the toy touches your device.

Does the toy need batteries or charging?

No. Each cubbie is a sturdy rubber figure with its chip sealed inside. The phone powers the chip when they touch, so there is nothing to charge or plug in.

What age is it for?

Ages 3 and up. The content is written by teachers and paced for little listeners, so it works for bedtime as well as busy mornings.
